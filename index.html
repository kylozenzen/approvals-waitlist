How it works

Buffer in. Approval out.

One small workflow between Buffer and your client. Buffer stays where you write and schedule.

  1. 01 IMPORT

    Sync from Buffer

    Tag the posts that need a yes. They come straight in, so there’s nothing to rebuild.

  2. 02 SEND

    Send one link

    Assign posts to a client and send their room. They open it without an account or a login.

  3. 03 APPROVE

    One person signs off

    Anyone on their team can comment. The final yes takes the owner’s 6-digit code.

  4. 04 RECORD

    Keep the receipt

    Every approval saves who, when, and the exact version they saw. The post stays in Buffer, ready to go.
